# 🍊 Orange: A Christ-Centered Tech Movement

> “In everything, glorify Jesus.” – 1 Corinthians 10:31

Orange is a Spirit-led mission to create open, ethical, and accessible technology that helps people grow in the knowledge and love of Jesus Christ. We are not just building devices — we are building digital discipleship tools for a lost and distracted world.

### ✝️ Why Orange?

Because the world is tired of greed, lock-in, data tracking, and control.  
Because people need tools that **draw them toward Christ**, not away from Him.  
Because Jesus is the only true Way, and we want every byte of code and circuit to point to Him.

---

## 🌍 Our Mission

To build the first open-source, foldable Christian smart device platform — called **Orange** — focused on:

- 📖 **Daily Devotion by Design** — First thing each day, every device opens to Scripture and prayer.
- 💻 **Open + Free Tech** — Devices people can own, customize, and use freely, without being tracked.
- 🛐 **Faith Tools** — Bible reading, journaling, online church, prayer sharing — all built-in.
- 📱 **All-in-One Foldable Device** — A scroll-inspired tri-fold device that functions as a phone, tablet, and desktop.
- 🤝 **Community of Believers** — Helping each other grow, serve, and prepare for eternity together.

---

## 💛 Our Values

- **Jesus First** — Every app, device, and feature must glorify Him
- **Freedom** — No lock-in, no ads, no manipulation
- **Access** — Devices built to be affordable or donated to those in need
- **Transparency** — Open source and open-hearted
- **Stewardship** — Tech as a tool, not a master

---

## 🔧 Phase 1: What We're Building First

1. A lightweight Linux-based OS prototype for Raspberry Pi or old PCs
2. Devotional launcher that opens to Scripture + prayer
3. Pre-installed Bible reading app (offline + ad-free)
4. Open GitHub project for believers to contribute
5. Daily “Orange Moments” — stories of how Jesus moves through tech & obedience
